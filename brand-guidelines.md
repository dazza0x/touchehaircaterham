## 1. Brand Story

Touché Hairdressing opened its doors in Caterham in 1985 — the mid-80s, an era of bold volume, crimping irons, and fearless style. For over 40 years we have styled our way through every major era of hair fashion: the supermodel blowouts and grunge of the '90s, the "Rachel" and sleek Y2K styles of the early 2000s, the balayage revolution of the 2010s, and the highly individual, expressive looks of today.

Through four decades of ever-changing trends, one thing has never changed: our commitment to exceptional hairdressing. We've built deep, lasting relationships with Caterham families — styling generation after generation — and we remain a proud cornerstone of the local community.

We believe real expertise comes from blending years of experience with a genuine passion for continuous learning. Our team regularly attends premier industry training so clients always leave in the best possible hands.

## 2. Brand Personality

Touché is **The Trusted Expert Next Door** — warm, accomplished, and deeply rooted in the community. Not a faceless chain, not a trendy pop-up. A proper local salon with four decades of craft behind it.

### The Four Traits

| Trait | What It Means | We Are | We Are Not |
|-------|--------------|--------|------------|
| **Assured** | Confident through experience, not ego | Expert, decisive, knowledgeable | Boastful, condescending, corporate |
| **Warm** | Genuinely caring about people and their hair | Welcoming, personal, community-minded | Gushing, over-familiar, salesy |
| **Crafted** | Proud of the work — the skill, the finish, the detail | Precise, considered, quality-obsessed | Rushed, trendy-for-trendy's-sake, gimmicky |
| **Rooted** | Grounded in Caterham and Surrey, part of the fabric | Local, trustworthy, consistent | Transient, anonymous, generic |

### Brand in One Sentence

> *A Caterham institution — expert stylists who take the time to get it right, every time.*

## 3. Voice & Tone

> For the full personality system — character archetype (Helen) and situational dial settings — see `brand-personality.md`.

### The Voice

Touché speaks like a confident, skilled professional who also happens to be genuinely lovely. The voice is never stiff or salesy — it's the tone of someone who knows their craft inside out and enjoys talking about it without showing off.

**Positioning on the spectrums:**

```
Formal  ←——————●———→  Casual         (slightly warm of centre)
Simple  ←————●—————→  Complex        (clear, never dumbed-down)
Serious ←——————●———→  Playful        (professional with warmth)
Reserved ←—————●————→  Expressive    (measured, not gushing)
```

### We Sound Like

- "Over 40 years in Caterham — we've seen every trend come and go. We know what works."
- "A proper consultation before every service. We want to understand your hair, your lifestyle, your goals."
- "From precision cuts to vivid colour transformations — you'll leave looking and feeling incredible."
- "Est. 1985 in the heart of Caterham. Still here, still the best."

### We Don't Sound Like

- "Hey babe! Obsessed with your new look! 😍😍😍" *(too casual, too social-media generic)*
- "Our cutting-edge, innovative techniques leverage the latest trends..." *(corporate jargon)*
- "Book NOW — limited slots!" *(pressure-selling; we don't do that)*
- "We're passionate about your journey" *(vague wellness-speak)*

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Website headlines | Bold, confident | *"Hair That Turns Heads."* |
| Website body copy | Warm, authoritative | *"We've been doing this since 1985 — so you're in safe hands."* |
| Service descriptions | Clear, benefit-led | *"Includes VAT, shampoo and a complimentary refreshment."* |
| Social media | Slightly warmer, conversational | *"Balayage done right — inside the salon today."* |
| Booking prompts | Direct, friction-free | *"Call 01883 340771 or book online."* |
| New client comms | Warm and reassuring | *"We'll start with a full consultation — no rush, no pressure."* |

### Words We Use

precision · craft · expertise · trusted · consultation · community · Caterham · decades · timeless · considered · results · welcoming · relaxed · stylists · technique

### Words We Avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Revolutionary / cutting-edge | Overused, empty |
| Journey / transformation | Wellness cliché |
| Passionate about | Vague and overused |
| Book NOW / limited slots | Pressure-selling |
| Seamless / synergy | Corporate jargon |
| Obsessed | Too social-media informal |
| Babe / hun | Too familiar for the brand register |

## 7. Logo & Wordmark

The Touché logo uses **Playfair Display** as the wordmark typeface. The accent on the "é" is part of the brand identity and must never be removed.

### Usage Rules

- Always render in `--gold` (`#bba996`) on dark backgrounds
- On light backgrounds, render in `--dark` (`#201d19`)
- Minimum digital size: 120px wide
- Clear space: equal to the cap-height of the wordmark on all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour outside the approved palette, or add effects

### Don'ts

- Don't write "Touche" without the accent
- Don't use Gold on Cream (insufficient contrast)
- Don't combine the wordmark with decorative drop shadows
- Don't place on busy photographic backgrounds without a dark overlay

## 8. Imagery Guidelines

### Photography Style

Touché imagery should feel **warm, professional, and real** — not over-styled or overly filtered.

| Dimension | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Lighting** | Soft, warm, natural or studio-natural — never harsh flash |
| **Subjects** | Real clients or stylists; authentic in-salon moments |
| **Colour treatment** | Warm tones consistent with the cream/gold palette; avoid cold blue casts |
| **Composition** | Clean backgrounds, focus on hair detail and finish |
| **Mood** | Relaxed confidence — a client who looks great and knows it |

### What to Show

- Finished looks: colour, cut, texture, shine
- In-salon atmosphere: styling in progress, stylist and client interaction
- Detail shots: balayage, highlights, smoothing finishes
- The salon environment: warm, professional, welcoming

### What to Avoid

- Stock photography that looks generic or corporate
- Heavily filtered or oversaturated edits
- Cold or clinical lighting
- Overly trendy editorial looks that don't reflect the actual service offering

## 11. Social Media

### Tone by Platform

| Platform | Register | Content focus |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| Facebook | Warm, community-led | Salon news, seasonal offers, client results (with permission) |
| Instagram | Visual-first, slightly aspirational | Finished looks, colour work, detail shots |

### Caption Formula

1. The result or moment (visual anchor)
2. One or two lines of voice-consistent copy
3. Soft CTA — "Book via the link" / "Call 01883 340771"
4. 3–5 relevant hashtags maximum

**Example:**
*"Sun-kissed balayage, done right. Warm, natural tones blended for a finish that grows out beautifully — no harsh lines, no guesswork.*
*Book your colour consultation: 01883 340771.*
*#ToucheHairdressing #BalayageCaterham #SurreyHair"*
