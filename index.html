A Caterham Story

Forty years on one street.

Touché opened on Station Avenue in 1985. We’re still there — same shopfront, same standards, quietly shaping the way Caterham looks for over four decades.

Over that time we’ve earned something quieter than accolades: the trust that makes a salon part of someone’s life, not just their calendar.
