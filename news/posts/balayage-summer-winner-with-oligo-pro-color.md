July is always a revealing month in hairdressing. The bookings tell you what clients actually want, not what they said they wanted in January. And right now, the answer is overwhelmingly the same: lighter, softer, lived-in colour that looks as though the sun did the work.

## Why Balayage Still Wins in Summer

There's a reason balayage hasn't been replaced by the next big thing. It works with the hair's natural growth pattern, it softens regrowth lines, and when done well, it looks effortless. This summer we've been using **Oligo Pro** colour to achieve exactly that; warm, dimensional blondes with depth at the root and brightness through the mid-lengths and ends.

The key is placement. Anyone can paint sections. The skill is reading the head shape, the natural fall of the hair, and the existing tone before a brush touches the hair. Oligo's colour range gives us the control to achieve clean, precise results without the harshness that cheaper processes can produce.

## Extensions: Technique Matters More Than the Method

There's been useful industry discussion this month about tape-in versus sew-in extension techniques and which delivers better results for clients. The honest answer is that neither method is universally superior. Both can be done brilliantly or badly. What matters is the consultation: understanding the client's hair density, lifestyle, and maintenance commitment before recommending any approach.

If you're considering extensions, ask detailed questions at your appointment. A good stylist will give you honest advice rather than default to whichever technique they find quickest.

## Dry Cutting — Underestimated, Increasingly Valued

The wider industry has been paying attention to dry cutting lately, with workshops and events drawing stylists specifically to sharpen this skill. We've always valued it here. Cutting hair dry lets you see exactly how the finished shape will behave, particularly important for clients with waves, curls, or fine hair that behaves very differently wet versus dry. It's not a trend. It's a technique that demands more precision, which is precisely why not every salon bothers with it.

## Keeping Your Hair Healthy Through the Summer

Whatever colour service or cut you have this month, summer maintenance is worth thinking about. UV exposure, salt water, and chlorine all affect hair condition, particularly colour-treated hair. **Organethic Pure Care** colour-safe shampoo and conditioner are genuinely worth using through the summer months. The formulation supports colour longevity and keeps the hair shaft hydrated without weighing it down.

Dry shampoo between washes helps too, though it's a temporary measure, not a substitute for a proper haircare routine.
