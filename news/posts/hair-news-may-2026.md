# What's Happening in Hair: May 2026

May tends to be the month clients start thinking seriously about summer — lighter colours, fresher cuts, a general sense of wanting a change. It's also a busy time in the industry, with new products launching and ongoing conversations about what actually makes a salon visit worthwhile. Here's a round-up of what's caught our attention this month.

## Getting Colour Right Starts Before You Sit Down

There's been a lot of discussion in professional circles lately about colour consultations — specifically, the gap between what a client brings in on their phone and what's actually achievable on their particular hair. Modern Salon has been running pieces on managing expectations and correction work, and it's something we think about here too.

The truth is, a thorough consultation isn't a formality. It's where good colour work begins. Factors like your natural base, any previous chemical services, your hair's condition, and even how much time you can commit to maintenance all shape what we'd recommend. If you're considering a significant colour change this summer, book a consultation first — ideally a separate appointment before any chemical work. It saves disappointment on both sides.

## The Colour Trends Actually Worth Considering

Stepping back from product ranges for a moment, what are clients actually asking for this spring? Across the board, we're seeing interest in:

- **Lived-in blondes** — softer roots, natural-looking dimension rather than uniform brightness
- **Copper and warm auburn tones** — particularly flattering as the light changes through summer
- **Low-maintenance balayage** — clients want colour that grows out gracefully, not something that looks neglected after eight weeks

None of these are new ideas, but they're enduring because they work. The best colour choices tend to complement your natural tone and fit your actual lifestyle.
