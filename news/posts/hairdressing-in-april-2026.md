April tends to be a useful moment to take stock. The post-Christmas rush is behind us, summer bookings are starting to come in, and clients are thinking about change. Here's what's worth knowing right now — whether you're a regular at Touché or simply curious about where the industry is heading.



\## Sustainability Is Moving from Aspiration to Practice



The conversation around greener salons has shifted noticeably this year. It's no longer about making a statement — it's about making sensible decisions that reduce waste, lower running costs, and reflect what many clients actually care about. We're talking about things like energy-efficient equipment, reduced water consumption at the backwash, and being more deliberate about the product lines we stock. Salons that approach this practically — rather than as a marketing exercise — tend to do it better and stick with it longer. At Touché, it informs how we think about product sourcing and day-to-day operations, quietly and without fanfare.



\## Scalp Health Remains Central



This isn't a passing trend. Scalp treatments have earned a permanent place on the menu because the results justify it. A healthy scalp genuinely affects how hair grows, how colour takes, and how styles hold. We're seeing continued interest in treatments that address dryness, sensitivity, and density — and clients are increasingly coming in with specific concerns rather than vague requests. That's a good thing. It means better conversations and more targeted results.



\## Texture and Movement Over Stiffness



For spring and into summer, the direction is away from anything that looks constructed. Soft layers, lived-in waves, and cuts that move naturally are consistently what clients are asking for — and what suits most people well, frankly. The techniques behind these results are more considered than they might appear. A cut that looks effortless usually involves precise graduation and careful personalisation to the individual's hair type.



\## The Value of a Consistent Relationship



One thread running through how the better salons operate right now is a focus on consistency — not just in technical results, but in the overall experience. Clients notice when their stylist remembers the details, when appointments run on time, when advice is honest rather than optimistic. These things compound over years. It's one of the reasons independent salons like Touché tend to hold their client relationships well. There's no anonymous turnover here — just the same faces, getting better at what they do.



\## Booking Ahead This Spring



If you're thinking about a colour refresh or a significant cut before summer, April is a sensible time to get something in the diary. We're already seeing the calendar fill for May and June. Give us a call or book online — we'll find a time that works.
