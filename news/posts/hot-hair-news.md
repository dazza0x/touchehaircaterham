# What's Happening in Hair Right Now

The hair industry rarely stands still. From trade shows setting new standards for professional education to conversations about how salons grow sustainably. There's plenty worth paying attention to this week. Here's what caught our eye, and what it means for clients and professionals alike.

## The Rise of the Express Add-On

Industry conversations are increasingly focused on short, targeted services that complement a main appointment. Things like a scalp treatment, a glossing service, or a quick toning pass. The appeal is mutual: clients leave with more than they came in for, and the salon uses its time well. At Touché, we've always thought along these lines. A scalp treatment using Oligo Professionnel's scalp care range, added onto a colour appointment, takes minutes and makes a measurable difference — particularly for anyone dealing with sensitivity or buildup. Worth asking about, if you haven't already.

## Education Is Having a Moment

Premiere Orlando 2026 has been widely described as a landmark event for professional beauty education. Larger attendance, stronger technical content, and a renewed emphasis on skills over spectacle. Closer to home, the UK's own appetite for proper craft training is just as strong. The apprenticeship route into hairdressing remains one of the most practical ways to build a real career. Combining hands-on salon experience with structured learning. At Touché, we take that seriously. Developing the next generation of stylists isn't an afterthought; it's part of how good salons stay good. An apprentice who learns in a working salon, handling real clients, real colour, real conversations and comes out with something a classroom alone can't provide.

## Celebrating Craft and Legacy

The Beauty Changes Lives Gala has been drawing attention this week, recognising professionals who've made a lasting contribution to the industry. It's a good reminder that hairdressing, done well, is a genuine vocation. The stylists being celebrated didn't build careers on trends alone, they built them on consistency, technical ability, and the trust of their clients. That's as true in Caterham as it is anywhere.

## Extensions Done Properly

There's been renewed industry focus on safer, smarter approaches to hair extension installation. Specifically around methods that protect the integrity of the natural hair. It's a conversation worth having openly. Extensions carried out without proper assessment of the hair's condition can cause real damage. If you're considering them, the most important question to ask is whether your stylist has been properly trained — not just in installation, but in aftercare and removal too.

## The Short Version

Good hairdressing comes down to trained hands, honest advice, and services that are chosen for a reason. Whether you're a client or someone considering a career in the industry, that foundation doesn't change.
