# Summer 2026: Colour and Style Worth Talking About

Every summer brings its own energy, and 2026 is no exception. What's different this year is how considered it all feels, less about chasing a single hero look, and more about finding the version of a trend that actually suits *you*. After a few years of very cool, very uniform blondes and muted tones, warmth is back in earnest. Across the board, clients are asking for more dimension, more life, and colour that moves with the light.

Here's what we're seeing — and doing — at Touché right now.

## Warm Blonde is Doing the Heavy Lifting

Honey, butter, golden wheat, call it what you like, but the era of ashy, flat blonde is firmly behind us. This summer's blondes are luminous rather than bright. Think placement that frames the face with lighter pieces, then drops into something richer and more natural towards the mid-lengths. It's a grown-up take on the sun-kissed effect, and it photographs beautifully without looking overdone in person.

We're using Oligo Pro's lightening and toning systems to achieve exactly this; the kind of warmth that reads as deliberate, not brassy.

## Brunettes Are Getting Their Moment

For years brunettes were told to "just add highlights". Not any more. Copper, cinnamon, and deep auburn tones are coming through strongly, and glossing techniques that add extraordinary shine to brown bases are in high demand. A well-placed gloss can transform a dull, tired brunette into something genuinely rich and complex with no dramatic re-colour required.

## Scalp Health is Part of the Conversation

This isn't a trend so much as a correction. Clients are arriving with drier, more reactive scalps, partly from over-washing, partly from years of product build-up. We're addressing this before we even talk about colour. Oligo Pro's scalp care range has become a regular part of our consultation process, particularly for anyone whose colour results have been inconsistent. A healthy scalp holds colour better. Full stop.

## Texture and Smoothness — at the Same Time

It sounds contradictory, but the most-requested finish this summer is what I'd call "controlled natural." Not bone straight, not a wild curl it's something in between. The Crown smoothing system has been particularly useful here. It calms frizz and gives shape without removing movement entirely, which is exactly what most clients want heading into humid summer months.

## Colour-Safe Maintenance Matters More Than You Think

Warm, luminous colour fades fastest when it isn't protected at home. We recommend Organethic Pure Care products to maintain tone and condition between appointments. It's gentle enough for frequent use, effective enough to make a genuine difference.
